# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are building an original open-world game in public. Every contributor, regardless of experience, identity, or background, should be able to learn, ship, and stay.

## Expected

- Assume good intent, then be precise.
- Critique code and design, not people.
- Credit asset authors and other contributors.
- Keep discussion on the game, the engines, and the work.

## Not allowed

- Harassment, hate, or personal attacks.
- Sharing others' private information.
- Copying Rockstar (or any other studio) names, maps, vehicles, logos, voices, or story.
- Spam, self-promo dumps, or "just star my repo" drive-by comments.

## Enforcement

Report problems by opening a GitHub issue titled `CoC` or emailing the maintainers listed on the repo. Maintainers may warn, edit, or ban as needed to keep the project usable.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
